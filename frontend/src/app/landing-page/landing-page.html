A little clarity

Questions before you get going?

Straight answers to what learners ask most.

How do I choose a driving school?

Open the schools list, filter by city and price, and compare ratings, features and reviews from real students.

Can I look around before creating an account?

Yes. Browsing schools is open to everyone. You only need an account when you are ready to apply.

Does DrivEase provide the lessons?

No. Lessons are taught by the driving school you choose. DrivEase helps you find it, apply and keep track of your progress.

Can I track my progress online?

Yes. Your dashboard shows your application status, your booked lessons and the hours you have completed.

I run a driving school. Where do I start?

Use the school login to open your dashboard. If your school isn't on DrivEase yet, contact us and we will help you get set up.
